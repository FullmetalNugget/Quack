Boss Concept

Tall, black shadowy creature.

6 arms total; 4 are active and float toward the player to attack.

Stage 1

Boss is mostly still.

Hands attack the player.

Stage 2

Hands attack the player.

Boss jumps toward the player’s last known location.

Player manipulates boss movement by positioning.

Stage 3 (Final Stage)

Boss starts flying.

Hands attack continues.

Falling boulders drop from the ceiling.

Spikes also fall from the boss (or arena) from any direction.

Absolute radiance vibe: visually epic and intense.

Spikes are fast but reactable—challenging without being unfair.

Mechanics / Tricks

Player can’t rely on their gun; must use environment (boulders) to deal damage.

Boss movement and attacks are designed so players must predict and manipulate positioning.

Stage 3 is chaotic but skill-based, inspired by Absrad’s spike mechanics.
